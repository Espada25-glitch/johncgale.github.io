John C. Gale

Lecturer in Marketing · UVA Darden
Adjunct Faculty · Georgetown McDonough
Part-time Lecturer · NYU Washington, DC

I am a marketing educator, researcher, and executive based in Purcellville, Virginia. I teach across MBA, MSBA, professional master’s, and undergraduate programs, connecting consumer psychology, customer analytics, and marketing strategy with the decisions leaders face in practice.

My research examines how AI assistants shape consumer discovery and brand visibility. With Luca Cian and Luc Wathieu, I coauthored “How to Get AI to Surface Your Brand” in Harvard Business Review. The work also informs my Darden teaching case, conference presentations, and book in progress.

I bring that research into the classroom through cases, experiments, and applied analysis. Students learn to evaluate emerging tools, test assumptions, and make defensible decisions as marketing changes.

Alongside teaching and research, I serve as Vice President, Group Account Director at Synchrony Financial’s Goldmind and Founder and Principal of Madison & Monroe, LLC.
