Continuing Research

Research in progress

The Fragmented Shelf: How Large Language Models Create Divergent Brand Visibility

Research with Luca Cian on differences in brand visibility across large language models. The project examines AI-mediated consumer discovery and its implications for brand strategy.

Completed observational studies document differences across tested models and questions; they do not establish that a particular optimization tactic causes greater brand inclusion. Planned longitudinal work and causal experiments will examine how patterns change over time and test possible explanations. Practitioner interviews are also in development.

My research connects three questions: which brands AI assistants recommend, how those recommendations represent brands, and how consumers evaluate the options they encounter. Answer engine optimization (AEO) concerns brand visibility in AI-generated answers; understanding its value also requires evidence about consumer trust and choice.

This continuing work informs new cases and classroom exercises, helping students distinguish observation from explanation, evaluate uncertainty, and adapt their marketing decisions as technologies evolve.
